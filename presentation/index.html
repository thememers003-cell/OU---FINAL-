Slide 01 • Problem Statement

The Problem with Traditional Campus Printing

College students frequently need to print assignments, records, resumes, notes, project reports, and certificates.

🏢 Traditional Campus Xerox Dependency
  • Small Number of Shops: Most students depend on just 1 or 2 Xerox / printing shops around campus.
  • Multi-Service Burden: These shops are not only handling printing — they also provide stationery, scanning, binding, lamination, and other services.
  • Peak-Hour Crowds: During peak hours, this creates large crowds and long waiting times.
  • Queue Waste: Students often have to wait in queues just to print a few pages.
⚙️ The 6-Step Manual Operator Process

The shop operator has to manually perform every step:

  • 1. Receive the file (via WhatsApp, USB, or Bluetooth)
  • 2. Open and inspect the document on their computer
  • 3. Manually select printing settings (B/W, color, pages, copies)
  • 4. Take payment (cash change or manual UPI checking)
  • 5. Print the document on local printers
  • 6. Collate and hand it over to the student
For simple printing jobs, this manual process takes several minutes per student.
⚠️
Key Issue: A simple printing requirement becomes a time-consuming process because students and multiple services are concentrated at one physical counter.

Slide 03 • Deep Problem Analysis

More Than Just a Printing Problem

The issue isn't simply the cost of printing. It is about Time + Accessibility + Operational Efficiency.

⏱️ The Disproportionate Time Equation

A student who needs only 5–10 pages may spend significantly more time:

  • Travelling to the shop: 10 to 15 minutes walk
  • Waiting in the queue: 20 to 30 minutes standing in line
  • Completing the transaction: 3 to 5 minutes manual billing
  • Actual printing time: Only 15–20 seconds!
Over 90% of student time is pure deadweight waiting and logistics!
🚨 High-Stakes Deadline Chaos

This becomes especially problematic during crucial academic windows:

  • Before exams: Hall tickets, formula sheets, notes
  • During assignment submissions: Batch-wise deadlines
  • During project reviews: Major & minor project reports
  • During record submissions: Lab certificates & observation sheets
  • During placement / application periods: Resumes & credentials
  • During college events: Delegate sheets & event passes
